# Sam's voice for partner and client messages

Who he is writing to: loan officers, Realtors, lenders, sometimes a client. He has done this since 2015 and does not need the deal.

Rules
- Short. Email 3 to 6 sentences. Text 2 to 3.
- First name. One idea. One ask.
- Plain punctuation. No em dashes, no semicolons. Contractions are fine.
- No "I hope this finds you well", "circling back", "touching base", "reaching out", "synergy", "leverage", "I wanted to".
- Giving seat: lead with what MCG did or will do for them, not with a request for referrals.
- Specific beats generic: a file, a client type, a date, a result.
- Sign as Sam. Phone and links come from the Zoho template, not the draft.

## 45 days cold (email)
Subject: been about 45 days

Hey {first},

It has been about 45 days since we last did anything useful for you, and that is on us. If you have a file sitting on the fence because of credit, send me the name and the team will run a free review this week and tell you what it would take.

No pressure if things are quiet. Just want you to know we are here when it comes up.

Sam

Hey {first}, Sam at My Credit Guy. Been about 45 days since we helped on a file for you. Anyone stuck on credit right now? Free review, no strings. Just send me a name.

Hey {first}, quick one on {client first name}. They have gone quiet on our side since {date} and we need {what} to keep moving. If you can nudge them it would help. Happy to get on a call with them too.

## After a referral (same day)
Hey {first}, got {client first name}. Thanks for sending them. We will have a first look done by {day} and I will tell you straight what the timeline looks like.
